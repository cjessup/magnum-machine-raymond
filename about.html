About Magnum Machine, Inc.

Magnum Machine, Inc, established in 1995, is a precision machine shop specializing in turning. Working in a wide variety of industries, we manufacture contacts, pins, bodies, housings, boots, insulators, etc. We work with many different types of materials including beryllium copper, OHFC copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel, kovar, boron, and teflon to name just a few.

Over the years, Magnum Machine has developed a strong business relationship with several metal and plastic suppliers, plating companies, heat-treat facilities, and tool suppliers. We purchase in bulk in order to give us the ability to control costs. We try to keep up on changing economic conditions in order to be sure we have the materials on hand and available for our customers needs and expectations. We offer in-house heat treating when required.

At Magnum Machine lot sizes vary from 1 to 10000 pieces with a normal lead time of 3 to 4 weeks. We also do larger quantities per customer request. Many of our customers have come to rely on Magnum Machine to turn around machined parts in small quantities complete with heat treating and plating if needed in a timely manner. We make every attempt to accommodate our customers needs. Requests for quotations are provided within a 24 hour period, and can be returned ASAP if requested.

Our main goal is to meet and/or exceed the needs of our customers to assure continued growth for both the customer and Magnum Machine. We look forward to satisfying your company's needs.
