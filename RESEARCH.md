# Magnum Machine, Inc. — redesign research

Confirmed line of business from the live site (https://www.magnumnh.com/): Magnum Machine, Inc. is a precision machine shop in Raymond, NH, established in 1995, specializing in turning. Linked service pages also cover precision CNC milling and engineering prototypes, from multi-axis Swiss-style lathes and Okuma lathes to a four-axis Okuma milling machine. The shop names contacts, pins, bodies, housings, boots, and insulators, and lists materials including beryllium copper, OHFC copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel, kovar, boron, and teflon, plus steel, copper, and plastics on the machining pages.

Live nav pages fetched: Homepage, About, Precision CNC Turning, Precision CNC Milling, Engineering Prototypes, Facilities List, Communications & Telecommunications (`comm.php`, also linked as “and Many More...”), News, Contact. Aerospace, Defense & Military, Semiconductor, Automotive, Electronics, and Medical pages still respond, but they are commented out of the live nav and repeat the same industries paragraph. That paragraph is kept on the industries page. Those hidden URLs are not separate demo pages.

Original palette and type: black page chrome (`#000`), cyan accent `#2fbbec` (wordmark bar, buttons, header rule), body text `#303030` / headings `#121212`, grays `#e7e7e7` and `#989898`, Open Sans and Open Sans Condensed. Imagery is shop-floor photos (Okuma equipment) and overhead part layouts. The wordmark is type, not a separate logo file: “Magnum” with “Machine Inc.” in a lighter weight on a cyan bar.

## Peer sites

Layout and interaction ideas only. No peer copy, photos, or branding are used.

### Owens Industries — https://www.owensind.com/

Precision CNC shop (milling, turning, mill-turn). Ideas borrowed:

- Photo-led opening, then the quote action immediately under it instead of a text overlay on the picture.
- Capabilities shown as a short visual set (turning, milling, prototypes) before long copy.
- Industries as a compact linked row, not a stack of essays.
- A second quote prompt at the end of the page.

### Pioneer Service — https://pioneerserviceinc.com/

Precision CNC Swiss machining and grinding shop. Ideas borrowed:

- A factual proof line near the top (years in the trade), then services, then industries.
- A tight row of shop facts in place of slogan badges. Magnum’s own lot size, lead time, quote window, and tolerances fill that row.
- A plain “send the drawing / request a quote” close for buyers who already know the part.

### Protomatic — https://www.protomatic.com/

Prototype and production CNC shop. Ideas borrowed:

- Early blocks for what buyers actually check: machining work, then inspection equipment, instead of one long brochure paragraph.
- Industry names as simple chips that return to one industries statement, because Magnum’s live industry pages share a single paragraph.
- Inspection (comparators, CMM, vision) treated as its own section beside the machine list.

Cer-Mac (https://www.cermac.com/) was reviewed and not used as a model. Its page is long-form local SEO. The only related pattern already required here is a persistent phone action.
