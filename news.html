Magnum Machine now ITAR Registered

Spring, 2013

Magnum Machine Inc is happy to announce that we are ITAR Registered. ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) is a program directed by the U.S State Department and is designed to control the import and export of U.S. Defense and military related articles and information. Magnum Machine can now fully support military and defense related projects in the United States.
